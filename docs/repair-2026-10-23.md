## Fixes

**Native Swift (AR capture)**
- The floor plane is frozen once it has been used.
- Candidates classified as non-floor, or above the camera, are excluded from floor selection.
- Floor taps intersect the saved plane even after ARKit removes its anchors.
- An explicit, localized, confirmed restart keeps the JPEGs and nulls the old poses. The late-photo generation guard is kept and tested.
- Three new scripts: `scripts/native-capture-stubs.swift`, `scripts/native-capture-tests.swift`, `scripts/native-capture-tests.py`.

**Capture web and storage**
- First-write reset-generation race fixed with a generation-only preflight that does not load photos.
- Stale native callbacks and file-read boundaries cancel and clean up their temporary files.
- Room flags are filtered to that room's questions. The current human answer or confirmation is shown, and nothing is auto-answered.

**Python pipeline**
- Duplicate JSON keys are rejected, and `RecursionError` is handled in a controlled way.
- Localhost is pinned to `127.0.0.1`, and an explicit `::1` is supported.
- A missing or partial calibration identity or config now fails before any photo loads or inference starts. Historical calibration without a model identity is still rejected. The documented capture command already uses `--no-calibration`.
